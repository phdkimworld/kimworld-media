EX-99.1 2 ex991.htm NEWS RELEASE DATED NOVEMBER 13, 2025 Exhibit 99.1 BTQ Technologies to Host Live Webinar on Quarterly Financial Results and General Corporate Update VANCOUVER, BC, Nov. 13, 2025 /CNW/ - BTQ Technologies Corp. ("BTQ" or the "Company") (Nasdaq: BTQ) (CBOE CA: BTQ) (FSE: NG3), a global quantum technology company focused on securing mission-critical networks, is pleased to announce that it will hold a shareholder call on Monday, November 17, 2025, at 12:00 p.m. EST to discuss its quarterly financial results and provide a general corporate update. The call will follow the release of the Company's Q3 2025 financial statements , which will be issued after market close on Friday, November 14, 2025 . About BTQ BTQ Technologies Corp. (Nasdaq: BTQ | Cboe CA: BTQ | FSE: NG3 ) is a vertically integrated quantum company accelerating the transition from classical networks to the quantum internet. Backed by a broad patent portfolio, BTQ pioneered the industry's first commercially significant quantum advantage and now delivers a full-stack, neutral-atom quantum computing platform with end-to-end hardware, middleware, and post-quantum security solutions for finance, telecommunications, logistics, life sciences, and defense.
