 Item 3.03. 
 Material Modifications to Rights of Security Holders. 
 As previously disclosed,
Strive, Inc. (the "Company") entered into an underwriting agreement (the “Underwriting Agreement”) with Barclays
Capital Inc. and Cantor Fitzgerald & Co., as the joint book-running managers and representatives of the several underwriters, relating
to the issuance and sale in an underwritten offering (the “Offering”) of 7,000,000 shares (the “Shares”) of the
Company’s 15.00% Series A Perpetual SATS Preferred Stock, $0.001 par value per share (the “SATS Stock”). 
 In connection with the
issuance of the SATS Stock, Strive filed a Certificate of Designation (the “Certificate of Designation”) with the Nevada Secretary
of State on October 19, 2026 designating 17,000,000 shares, and establishing the terms, of the SATS Stock. 
 The SATS Stock accumulates
cumulative dividends (“regular dividends”) at a rate per annum equal to 15.00% on the stated amount of $100 per share (the
“stated amount”) thereof. Regular dividends will be payable when, as and if declared by Strive’s board of directors
(“Board”) or any duly authorized committee thereof, out of funds legally available for their payment, in arrears for each
regular dividend period on each regular dividend payment date to stockholders of record as of the close of business on the regular record
date immediately preceding the applicable regular dividend payment date. Regular dividend dates will fall on each business day, as defined
in the governing documentation for the SATS Stock, beginning on November 9, 2026. If declared in full, the first scheduled regular dividend
on the SATS Stock payable will include all accrued and unpaid dividends from and after the closing date. No dividend, interest or other
amount will accumulate or accrue on any unpaid regular dividends, regardless of whether regular dividends are declared for any future
regular dividend period. Declared regular dividends on the SATS Stock will be payable solely in cash in the manner, and subject to the
provisions, described in the Certificate of Designation. 
 At the closing of the
Offering, Strive increased its existing dividend reserve in an amount equal to the first 18 months of dividend payments calculated as
of the date of the Offering and deposited an equivalent of $22.50 per share of SATS Stock into an account funded by the Company with a
combination of existing cash on hand and securities (based on the value of such securities at the closing of this Offering). 
 The SATS Stock has a
par value of $0.001 per share and initially has a liquidation preference of $100 per share, subject to adjustment as set forth below (the
“Liquidation Preference”). The SATS Stock ranks senior to Strive’s Class A common stock, $0.001 par value per share
(the “Class A Common Stock”) and Class B common stock, $0.001 par value per share (the “Class B Common Stock”),
with respect to the payment of dividends and the distribution of assets upon Strive’s liquidation, dissolution or winding up. If
Strive liquidates, dissolves or winds up, whether voluntarily or involuntarily, then the holders of SATS Stock will be entitled to receive
payment for the Liquidation Preference of, and all accumulated and unpaid regular dividends and any compounded dividends on, their shares
of SATS Stock out of Strive’s assets or funds legally available for distribution to its stockholders, before any such assets or
funds are distributed to, or set aside for the benefit of, holders of the Class A Common Stock and Class B Common Stock or other junior
stock. The SATS Stock is junior to Strive’s Variable Rate Perpetual Preferred Stock (the “SATA Stock”) existing and
future indebtedness and structurally junior to the liabilities of Strive’s subsidiaries and subject to the rights and preferences
of any other class or series of preferred stock then outstanding. 
 The Liquidation Preference of the SATS Stock is
initially $100 per share; provided, however, that, effective immediately after the close of business on each business day after the initial
issue date (and, if applicable, during the course of a business day on which any sale transaction to be settled by the issuance of SATS
Stock is executed, from the exact time of the first such sale transaction during such business day until the close of business of such
business day), the Liquidation Preference per share of SATS Stock will be adjusted to be the greatest of (i) the stated amount per share
of SATS Stock; (ii) in the case of any business day with respect to which Strive has, on such business day or any business day during
the ten trading day period preceding such business day, executed any sale transaction to be settled by the issuance of SATS Stock, an
amount equal to the last reported sale price per share of SATS Stock on the trading day immediately before such business day; and (iii)
the arithmetic average of the last reported sale prices per share of SATS Stock for each trading day of the ten consecutive trading days
(or, if applicable, the lesser number of trading days as have elapsed during the period from, and including, the initial issue date to,
but excluding, such business day) immediately preceding such business day. 
 Strive has the right,
at its election, to redeem all, but not less than all, of the SATS Stock, at any time, for cash if the total number of shares of all SATS
Stock then outstanding is less than 25% of the total number of shares of SATS Stock originally issued in the Offering and in any future
offering, taken together. In addition, Strive has the right to redeem all, but not less than all, of the SATS Stock if certain tax events
occur. The redemption price for any SATS Stock to be redeemed will be a cash amount equal to the Liquidation Preference of the SATS Stock
to be redeemed as of the business day before the date on which Strive provides the related redemption notice, plus accumulated and unpaid
regular dividends, if any, thereon to, and including, the redemption date. 
 If an event that constitutes
a “Fundamental Change” under the Certificate of Designation governing the SATS Stock occurs, then holders of the SATS Stock
will have the right to require Strive to repurchase some or all of their shares of SATS Stock at a cash repurchase price equal to the
stated amount of the SATS Stock to be repurchased, plus accumulated and unpaid regular dividends, if any, to, and including, the Fundamental
Change repurchase date. 
 The SATS Stock has voting
rights with respect to certain amendments to Strive’s articles of incorporation or the Certificate of Designation, certain business
combination transactions and certain other matters. However, holders of the SATS Stock will not always be entitled to vote with holders
of Class A Common Stock on matters on which holders of Class A Common Stock are entitled to vote. 
 The above description
of the Certificate of Designation and the terms of the SATS Stock is a summary and is not complete. A copy of the Certificate of Designation
and the form of the certificate representing the SATS Stock are filed as Exhibits 3.1 and 4.1, respectively, to this Current Report on
Form 8-K, and the above summary is qualified by reference to the terms of the Certificate of Designation and the SATS Stock set forth
in such exhibits.
