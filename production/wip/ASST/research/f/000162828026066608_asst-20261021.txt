Other Events. Dividend Rate on SATS On October 21, 2026, Strive, Inc. ("Strive" or the "Company") announced the declaration by the board of directors of the Company of the first cash dividends on the Company’s 15.00% Series A Perpetual SATS Preferred Stock (the "SATS Stock"). The SATS Stock accrues cumulative dividends at a fixed rate of 15.00% per annum on the $100.00 stated amount per share, as further detailed in the governing documentation for the SATS Stock. Cash Dividend Declaration: Initial Dividend Periods Strive announced that its board of directors declared a cash dividend of $0.8710 per share (comprised of $0.4762 per share of SATS Stock for the portion of the initial dividend period from October 21, 2026 to and including October 31, 2026 (8 business days), and $0.3947 per share of SATS Stock for portion of the initial dividend period from November 1, 2026 to and including November 9, 2026 (6 business days)), representing a per annum dividend rate of 15.00% on the SATS Stock as calculated in the governing documentation for the SATS Stock. The initial dividend will be paid on November 9, 2026 to stockholders of record of SATS Stock at the close of business on November 6, 2026 ($0.8710 per share in the aggregate).
