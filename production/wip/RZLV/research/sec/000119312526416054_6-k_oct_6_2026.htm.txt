Non-GAAP Financial Measures. This report on Form 6-K and the exhibits hereto include adjusted EBITDA, which is a non-GAAP financial measure that Rezolve uses to assess underlying operating performance. It represents EBITDA adjusted for certain non-cash, non-recurring and other items, including share-based compensation, foreign exchange effects, certain fair-value and financing-related items, and specified acquisition, restructuring and other one-time costs. Net income (loss) is the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure to forward-looking Adjusted EBITDA. The Company is unable to provide a quantitative reconciliation of Adjusted EBITDA to net income (loss) without unreasonable efforts because it cannot predict with sufficient certainty the type and extent of specific reconciling items that would be needed to provide such a reconciliation.
