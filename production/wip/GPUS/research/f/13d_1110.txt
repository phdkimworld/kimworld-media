Contracts, Arrangements, Understandings or Relationships With Respect to Securities of the Issuer On January 29, 2026, the Issuer entered into a Securities Purchase Agreement (the "Purchase Agreement") with Ault Lending and certain other investors (the "Investors") pursuant to which the Issuer agreed to issue and sell to the Investors units (each, a "Unit") comprised of (i) one-tenth of a Class C ordinary share, $0.008 par value per share, of the Issuer (a "Class C Ordinary Share") and (ii) a warrant to purchase Shares (a "Warrant"), for a purchase price of $2.89 per Unit. On January 30, 2026 (the "Closing Date"), subject to the terms and conditions of the Purchase Agreement, the Issuer issued to Ault Lending Units representing (i) an aggregate of 120,482 Class C Ordinary Shares and (ii) Warrants to purchase an aggregate of 342,361 Shares, all of which Warrants were subsequently exercised on a net exercise basis by Ault Lending to purchase 342,361 such Shares. In connection with the Purchase Agreement, the Issuer entered into a registration rights agreement (the "Registration Rights Agreement") with Ault Lending and the other Investors pursuant to which the Issuer has agreed that, no later than twenty days after the Closing Date, the Issuer will file with the SEC a registration statement registering the resale of (a) the Shares issuable upon conversion of the Class C Ordinary Shares and (b) the Shares underlying the Warrants. The Issuer agreed to use its reasonable best efforts to have such registration statement declared effective as soon as practicable after the filing thereof and maintain the effectiveness of such registration statement. The foregoing is not a complete description of the Purchase Agreement, the Warrants and the Registration Rights Agreement, and are subject to, and each is qualified by reference to the full text and terms of the Warrants, the Purchase Agreement and the Registration Rights Agreement, the forms of which are attached hereto as Exhibits 99.1, 99.2 and 99.3, respectively, and incorporated herein by reference. The foregoing is not a complete description of the SPAA, and is subject to, and is qualified by reference to the full text and terms of the SPAA, the form of which is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.4, and incorporated herein by reference.
