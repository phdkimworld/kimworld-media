 On November 18, 2026, SEALSQ Corp (the “Company”)
issued a press release announcing that it had signed a non-binding letter of intent with QuintessenceLabs Pty. Ltd. (“QuintessenceLabs”),
an Australian quantum cybersecurity company, regarding the Company’s proposed acquisition of 100% of the equity interests of QuintessenceLabs.
A copy of the press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this report on Form 6-K. 
 In addition, the Company has relocated its principal
place of management and principal executive offices to Esplanade de Pont-Rouge 4, 1212 Grand-Lancy, Switzerland, and has been operating
from the new premises since on or about November 9, 2026. 
 The information contained in this Report on Form
6-K, including Exhibit 99.1 but excluding (i) the quotes of Carlos Moreira and Dr. Vikram Sharma, (ii) the information under the heading
“Strategic synergies,” and (iii) the information under the heading “About QuintessenceLabs” contained in Exhibit
99.1, is hereby incorporated by reference into (i) the registration statement on Form F-3 of the Company (File No. 333-286098), as
supplemented by the registration statement on Form F-3 filed pursuant to Rule 462(b) under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (File
No. 333-290874), (ii) the registration statement on Form F-3 of the Company (File No. 333-290963), as amended, and (iii) the registration
statement on Form S-8 of the Company (File No. 333-287139), and into the base prospectus and any prospectus supplement outstanding
under each of the foregoing registration statements, to the extent not superseded by documents or reports subsequently filed or furnished
