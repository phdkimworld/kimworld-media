These agreements add more than 1,000 megawatts of new, emissions-free generation to the grid and help ensure the continued operations of existing clean power for generations to come. The new megawatts, which are the equivalent of a new large-scale nuclear plant, are coming online with no costs being borne by existing customers,” said Joe Dominguez, chairman, president and chief executive officer of Constellation. “We’re building on that progress with Peak+, an integrated solution that brings together new capacity, including new generation and demand response, with energy products to help large customers meet their evolving needs. Our approach proves we can power the growing digital economy while strengthening our electric grid – all with private investment.” 1 “Third quarter adjusted operating earnings were $5.06 per share, compared with $3.04 a year ago. This strong performance allowed us to raise the midpoint of our adjusted operating earnings guidance range for the second time this year,” said Shane Smith, executive vice president and chief financial officer of Constellation. “Our disciplined capital allocation strategy, including accretive organic growth through nuclear uprates, the acquisition of Rhode Island State Energy Center and our continued aggressive pace of share repurchases, positions us to deliver sustained earnings growth.” Third Quarter 2026 Our GAAP Net Income for the third quarter of 2026 increased to $5.19 per share from $2.97 per share in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted (non-GAAP) Operating Earnings for the third quarter of 2026 increased to $5.06 per share from $3.04 per share in the third quarter of 2025. For the reconciliations of GAAP Net Income (Loss) to Adjusted (non-GAAP) Operating Earnings, refer to the GAAP/Adjusted (non-GAAP) Operating Earnings Reconciliation section below. Adjusted (non-GAAP) Operating Earnings in the third quarter of 2026 primarily reflects: • Favorable market and portfolio conditions and addition of Calpine, partially offset by unfavorable interest and nuclear PTC portfolio Recent Developments and Third Quarter Highlights • Agreement to Acquire Rhode Island State Energy Center: We entered into an agreement to acquire all of the equity interests in RISEC Holdings, LLC, the owner of the Rhode Island State Energy Center, from Shell Energy North America (US), L.P. for $715 million, subject to customary purchase price adjustments. The Rhode Island State Energy Center is a natural gas-fired combined-cycle electric generation facility with a generating capacity of 609 megawatts (MW). The facility sells electricity and capacity into the competitive ISO New England wholesale power market and is expected to become part of our merchant generation portfolio. Completion of the transaction is subject to receipt of required regulatory approvals and other customary closing conditions. • Long-Term Agreement with Amazon Supporting Calvert Cliffs Clean Energy Center Investment and Expansion: In September 2026, we announced a 20-year, 690 MW PPA with Amazon and a related retail supply agreement supporting Amazon's operations in PJM. The agreement will enable more than $3 billion in infrastructure investment in our Calvert Cliffs Clean Energy Center, including facility improvements and a 190 MW capacity expansion expected to come online between 2030 and 2032, while supporting the facility's continued operation and future relicensing. • Long-Term Collaboration with Google Supporting New Nuclear Capacity in the PJM Grid: In October 2026, we announced a 20-year PPA with Google that will enable more than $4.3 billion in investment across 11 of our nuclear units in Illinois, Pennsylvania, and New Jersey to add approximately 890 MW of incremental generation capacity to the PJM grid, with the first uprate expected in 2028. The loan would have a 30-year term.
