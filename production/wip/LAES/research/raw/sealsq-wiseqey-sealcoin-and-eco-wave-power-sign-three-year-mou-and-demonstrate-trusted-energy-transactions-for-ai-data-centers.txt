SEALSQ, WISeQey, SEALCOIN and Eco Wave Power Sign Three-Year MoU and Demonstrate Trusted Energy Transactions for AI Data Centers
Collaboration combines renewable wave-energy production, secure digital identities and automated transactions, with potential applications for AI data centers and industrial energy buyers.
– SEALSQ Corp (NASDAQ: LAES) ("SEALSQ" or "Company"), a company that focuses on developing and selling Semiconductors, PKI, and Post-Quantum technology hardware and software products, today announced that together with its parent company, WISeQey Corp. (“WISeQey”) (Nasdaq/SIX: WQEY), a global quantum cybersecurity and space IoT company and sister company SEALCOIN AG, has entered into a three-year Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Eco Wave Power U.S. Inc., a wholly owned subsidiary of Eco Wave Power Global AB (Nasdaq: WAVE).
The collaboration brings together Eco Wave Power's renewable-energy technology, SEALSQ's secure semiconductors, WISeQey's digital identity expertise and SEALCOIN's machine communication and transaction infrastructure to explore how renewable-energy assets can securely interact with digital services and potential energy buyers.
Using Eco Wave Power's wave-energy installation in Jaffa Port, Israel, the companies have demonstrated:
Connecting physical energy production to an authenticated digital representation, allowing the origin and integrity of production data to be verified.
Using trusted production data to initiate a secure, authorized and auditable transaction between known counterparties under predefined commercial conditions.
The demonstration connects physical energy production with digital identity, secure communications and automated transactions, establishing a foundation for future machine-to-machine commercial interactions.

Connecting Renewable Energy to AI Infrastructure
Under the three-year MoU, the parties intend to further develop and evaluate these capabilities for potential commercial applications, particularly connecting renewable-energy producers with
AI data centers, industrial facilities, energy aggregators and utilities
As electricity demand from AI infrastructure grows, trusted digital systems could help energy producers make authenticated production information available to qualified buyers and automate transactions under agreed commercial conditions.
Eco Wave Power has initiated discussions with data center developers and infrastructure partners to explore potential future electricity supply opportunities. Eco Wave Power U.S., its AI and data center arm and a member of the NVIDIA Inception program, will lead its participation in the collaboration.
Carlos Moreira, Founder and CEO of SEALSQ, commented: “This collaboration creates an opportunity to extend the WISeQey ecosystem into renewable energy, a market where secure connectivity and trusted transactions can support new digital services. By combining SEALSQ’s secure semiconductors, WISeQey’s digital identity and PKI expertise, and SEALCOIN’s decentralized communication and transaction infrastructure with Eco Wave Power’s renewable-energy technology, we aim to demonstrate how our complementary capabilities can address real-world infrastructure needs. Our focus is on validating practical applications that could create new commercial opportunities across the ecosystem.”
Inna Braverman, Founder and CEO of Eco Wave Power, commented: “Building the energy infrastructure for AI requires both new sources of electricity and trusted systems capable of connecting those sources with customers. Our ambition is to bring wave energy into that ecosystem. Together with SEALCOIN, SEALSQ and WISeQey, we have demonstrated how physical wave-energy production can become trusted digital information and initiate a secure, auditable transaction.
We believe this creates an important foundation for a future in which wave-energy producers can securely connect with AI data centers and other energy buyers, transact under agreed commercial terms and ultimately participate directly in the digital energy economy.
Jonathan Llamas, Chief Product & Strategy Officer of SEALCOIN, commented: “Until now, machines have primarily been designed to exchange information. We are taking this a step further by enabling them to turn trusted physical-world data into autonomous economic actions. With Eco Wave Power, we have demonstrated how authenticated energy production can feed a digital twin and trigger a secure, auditable transaction between authorized parties.”
An Integrated Technology Platform with a Post-Quantum Roadmap
The collaboration combines SEALSQ's hardware security, WISeQey's digital identity and Public Key Infrastructure (PKI), SEALCOIN's trusted machine communication and transaction capabilities, and Eco Wave Power's real-world renewable-energy assets. Together, these technologies establish an architecture connecting physical infrastructure to authenticated digital information and auditable transactions.
The companies also intend to explore future integration of SEALSQ's QS7001 post-quantum secure element and quantum-resistant cryptographic technologies, supporting the long-term security requirements of critical infrastructure.

Eco Wave Power Global (NASDAQ: WAVE) is a leading onshore wave energy company that converts ocean and sea waves into clean, reliable, and cost-efficient electricity using its patented and intelligent technology. By generating renewable power directly from existing coastal infrastructure such as breakwaters, jetties, and piers, Eco Wave Power enables sustainable electricity production in close proximity to coastal cities, ports, and energy-intensive infrastructure.
As global electricity demand continues to rise with the growth of artificial intelligence, digital infrastructure, and next-generation data centers - often referred to as “AI factories” - Eco Wave Power’s technology is designed to help provide renewable energy near shorelines where many data centers, industrial facilities, and population centers are located.
With a mission to accelerate the global transition to renewable energy while supporting the next generation of digital and industrial infrastructure, Eco Wave Power developed and operates Israel’s first grid-connected wave energy power station, recognized as a “Pioneering Technology” by the Israeli Ministry of Energy and co-funded by EDF Power Solutions. In the United States, the Company recently launched the first-ever onshore wave energy pilot station at the Port of Los Angeles, in collaboration with Shell Marine Renewable Energy.
Eco Wave Power is expanding globally with projects planned in Portugal, Taiwan, and India, representing a project pipeline of 404.7 MW. The Company has received international recognition and support from organizations including the European Union Regional Development Fund, Innovate UK, and the EU Horizon 2020 program, and was honored with the United Nations Global Climate Action Award.

Each subsidiary contributes to WISeQey’s mission of securing the internet while focusing on their respective areas of research and expertise. Trusted by the OISTE/WISeQey cryptographic Root of Trust, WISeQey provides secure authentication and identification for IoT, blockchain, and AI applications. The WISeQey Root of Trust ensures the integrity of online transactions between objects and people.
