<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0"><B>Admission of Ordinary Shares to Trading on Euronext Growth Paris</B></P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">On November 25, 2026, SEALSQ Corp (the &ldquo;Company&rdquo; or &ldquo;SEALSQ&rdquo;)
announced that Euronext Paris has approved the admission (the &ldquo;Admission&rdquo;) of the Company&rsquo;s existing ordinary shares,
par value USD 0.01 per share (the &ldquo;Ordinary Shares&rdquo;), to trading on Euronext Growth Paris by way of direct admission, as a
secondary listing alongside the primary listing of the Company&rsquo;s Ordinary Shares on the Nasdaq Global Select Market. Trading of
the Ordinary Shares on Euronext Growth Paris is expected to begin on December 2, 2026, under the ticker symbol &ldquo;LAES&rdquo; and
ISIN VGG794831062. A copy of the press release is furnished as Exhibit 99.1 to this Report on Form 6-K.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">No new Ordinary Shares will be issued, no securities will be offered
or sold, and the Company will receive no proceeds in connection with the Admission. The Ordinary Shares will continue to be listed on
the Nasdaq Global Select Market, and the Company will remain subject to reporting obligations under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934,
as amended, and the rules of the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the &ldquo;SEC&rdquo;), as well as the Nasdaq Listing Rules. Following
the Admission, the Company will also be subject to the Euronext Growth Markets Rule Book and to Regulation (EU) No 596/2014 on market
abuse.</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">&nbsp;</P>

<P STYLE="font: 10pt Times New Roman, Times, Serif; margin: 0pt 0">This Report on Form 6-K, including Exhibit 99.1, does not constitute
an offer to sell or the solicitation of an offer to buy any securities in the United States, the European Economic Area or any other jurisdiction.</P>

of its existing ordinary shares (the &ldquo;Ordinary Shares&rdquo;) for trading on Euronext Growth Paris, as a secondary listing, alongside
the Company&rsquo;s primary listing of Ordinary Shares on the Nasdaq Global Select Market. Trading on Euronext Growth Paris is expected to begin
on December 2, 2026, under the ticker symbol &ldquo;LAES&rdquo; and ISIN VGG794831062. SEALSQ will mark the first day of trading with an
opening bell ceremony at Euronext Paris.</P>
