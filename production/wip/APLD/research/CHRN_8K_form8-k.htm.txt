 Item 1.01. Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement. 
 The
Private Placements 
 On
November 18, 2026, ChronoScale Holdings Corporation, a Nevada corporation (the “Company”), entered into definitive
agreements for two independent, concurrent private placement offerings: (i) a securities purchase agreement (the “Common Stock
Purchase Agreement”) with the purchasers named therein (the “Common Stock Purchasers”) for the private placement (the
“Common Stock Private Placement”) of shares (the “Shares”) of the Company’s common stock, par value $0.001
per share (the “Common Stock”), at a purchase price of $8.00 per Share, and (ii) a preferred equity purchase agreement (the
“PEPA,” and together with the Common Stock Purchase Agreement, the “Purchase Agreements”), with the investor
signatory thereto (the “Investor,” and together with the Common Stock Purchasers, the “Purchasers”) for the private
placement from time to time (the “Preferred Stock Private Placement,” and together with the Common Stock Private Placement,
the “Private Placements”) of up to an aggregate of $500,000,000 (the “Commitment Amount”) of newly designated
Series C Preferred Stock, par value $0.001 per share (the “Series C Preferred Stock”), subject to certain conditions and
limitations, at a purchase price per share equal to the Stated Value (as defined in Item 5.03 below) divided by 103%. Goldman Sachs &
Co. LLC is serving as exclusive capital markets advisor to the Company and is acting as lead placement agent for the Private
Placements. Northland Capital Markets is acting as co-placement agent for the Private Placements. Lake Street Capital Markets and B.
Riley Securities are serving as senior financial advisors, and Lucid Capital Markets and Needham & Company are serving as financial
advisors, to the Company. The Shares and the shares of Series C Preferred Stock will be sold in two separate Private Placements
in reliance on the exemption from registration provided by Section 4(a)(2) of the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities
Act”). 
 The Company expects to close on
the transactions under the Common Stock Purchase Agreement on November 18, 2026, and expects to receive $153.0 million in gross proceeds in consideration of approximately 19.1 million shares of Common Stock. The Company expects to deliver an initial put notice to the Investor for the purchase of shares of Series C
Preferred Stock having an aggregate purchase price of $10.0 million on November 18, 2026.
 The
Company intends to use the net proceeds from the Common Stock Private Placement to fund its capital commitment to the Partnership (as
defined below), which includes, among other things, the construction and development of a data center project located in Odessa,
Texas (the “Project”) and the purchase of GPUs for deployment at the Project, and for general corporate purposes. The Company
intends to use the net proceeds from the Preferred Stock Private Placement for working capital and general corporate purposes. 
 Each
Purchase Agreement contains customary representations, warranties and agreements by the Company and the applicable Purchasers, indemnification
obligations of the Company (and, in the case of the PEPA, the Investor) and other obligations of the parties. The representations, warranties
and covenants contained in the Purchase Agreements were made only for purposes of such agreements and as of the dates specified therein;
are solely for the benefit of the parties thereto (except as specifically set forth therein); may be subject to qualifications and limitations
agreed upon by the parties in connection with negotiating such agreements, instead of establishing matters as facts; and may be subject
to standards of materiality applicable to the contracting parties that differ from those applicable to investors generally; and should
not be relied upon as characterizations of the actual state of facts or condition of the Company. 
 Under
the Common Stock Purchase Agreement, the Company agreed, subject to certain exceptions for exempt issuances, not to issue, enter into
any agreement to issue or announce the issuance of any shares of Common Stock or Common Stock equivalents, or file any registration statement
(other than as contemplated by the Registration Rights Agreement (as defined below)), from the date of the Common Stock Purchase Agreement
until 30 days from the effective date of the Registration Statement (as defined below). In addition, the Company’s directors, officers
and 10% stockholders entered into lock-up agreements with the Placement Agent (the
“Lock-Up Agreements”) restricting transfers of their shares of Common Stock for a period of 30 days from the effective date
of the Registration Statement, subject to customary exceptions.

 GPU
Services Agreement 
 At
the Contribution Closing, the Partnership will
become the provider under an Amended and Restated GPU Services Agreement (Order #1), including its Terms of Service and related exhibits
and schedules (the “GPU Services Agreement”), with a leading frontier artificial intelligence lab (the “Customer”).
The Partnership will assume the GPU Services Agreement by assignment and novation from STN. Under the GPU Services Agreement,
the Partnership will provide the Customer with dedicated GPU services at the Project, with a service term of approximately six years
for each tranche (subject to extension at the Customer’s option). The GPU services will be made available to the Customer in four
tranches. The GPU Services Agreement also contemplates that, if agreed by the parties in writing, the Partnership may deploy optional
additional tranches of GPU capacity at the Project for the Customer. 
 The
first tranche is expected to be delivered in the fourth quarter of calendar 2026, and the remaining tranches are expected
to be delivered in the first half of calendar year 2027 (subject to extension in certain circumstances). A substantial portion of the
first phase is backstopped by credit support from an investment-grade technology company, and the GPU Services Agreement is expected
to provide up to $7.5 billion in total contract value to the Partnership over approximately six years, assuming full performance and
delivery of the contracted capacity and excluding any extension periods and the additional tranches. 
 The
GPU Services Agreement may be terminated by the Customer under certain circumstances, including, with respect to affected tranches, delayed
delivery and sustained service level failures, for which cure rights may be available. The Partnership’s termination rights are
more limited, and neither party may terminate for convenience. 
 The
GPU Services Agreement also contains other customary provisions for an agreement of this nature, including acceptance testing, service
level commitments, delay discounts, security requirements, representations and warranties, force majeure, indemnities, limitations of
liability, change of control provisions and provisions relating to the financing of the GPU systems.

5.03. Amendments to Articles of Incorporation or Bylaws; Change in Fiscal Year. 
 On
November 16, 2026, the Company filed with the Secretary of State of the State of Nevada a Certificate of Designations of the Powers,
Preferences and Relative, Participating, Optional and Other Restrictions of Series C Convertible Preferred Stock (the “Certificate
of Designation”), designating 500,000 shares of the Company’s authorized but unissued preferred stock as Series C Preferred
Stock, with a stated value of $1,000 per share (the “Stated Value”). The following is a summary of the principal terms of
the Series C Preferred Stock as set forth in the Certificate of Designation: 
 Ranking;
Liquidation. The Series C Preferred Stock ranks senior to the Common Stock, on parity with any future class or series of equity securities
that by its terms ranks on parity with the Series C Preferred Stock, junior to any class or series of equity securities that by its terms
ranks senior to the Series C Preferred Stock, and junior to all existing and future indebtedness of the Company. Upon any liquidation,
dissolution or winding up of the Company, holders of the Series C Preferred Stock will be entitled to receive, after payment of the Company’s
debts and other liabilities and before any distribution to holders of Common Stock, an amount per share equal to the Stated Value plus
any accrued but unpaid dividends. 
 Dividends;
Voting. Holders of the Series C Preferred Stock are entitled to participate, on an as-converted basis (without regard to any limitations
on conversion), in any dividends or distributions paid to holders of Common Stock. The Series C Preferred Stock has no voting rights,
except as required by law and for certain customary protective provisions requiring the consent of the holders of a majority of the outstanding
shares of Series C Preferred Stock. 
 Conversion. 
Each share of Series C Preferred Stock will be convertible at the option of the holder, at any time after the registration statement
registering the shares of Common Stock issuable upon conversion of the Series C Preferred Stock has been declared effective by
the SEC, into a number of shares of Common Stock equal to the Stated Value divided by the conversion price in effect on the conversion
date (the “Conversion Price”). The Conversion Price will equal the greater of (i) 95% of the lowest volume weighted average
price of the Common Stock during the five trading days immediately preceding the conversion date and (ii) the floor price, initially
$12.00 (as may be adjusted from time to time, the “Floor Price”), provided that in no event will the Conversion Price exceed
the ceiling price, initially $25.00 (as may be adjusted from time to time, the “Ceiling Price”). The Company’s Board
of Directors may, in its sole discretion, increase or reduce the Floor Price (but not below $3.60) or the Ceiling Price at any
time. 
 Conversion
Limitations. A holder may not convert shares of Series C Preferred Stock to the extent that, after giving effect to such conversion,
the holder (together with its attribution parties) would beneficially own in excess of 4.99% of the outstanding shares of Common Stock
(which percentage a holder may increase or decrease upon written notice to the Company, provided that any increase will not be effective
until the 61st day after such notice and in no event may such percentage exceed 9.99%). Conversions are also subject to the PEPA Exchange
Cap. If any holder is prevented from converting any portion of its Series C Preferred Stock because of the PEPA Exchange Cap, and such
limitation continues through the earlier of the date that is 18 months following the issuance of such Series C Preferred Stock and the
date that is 36 months following the date of the PEPA, then such portion will be redeemed by the Company in cash, within ten trading
days after such date, at a price equal to the greater of (A) the product of (x) the Conversion Price in effect on the first date such
limitation prohibited conversion and (y) the number of shares of Common Stock into which such Series C Preferred Stock is convertible
at such Conversion Price (without regard to any limitations on conversion), and (B) 110% of the Stated Value of such Series C Preferred
Stock. 
 Trading
Failure Redemption. If the Common Stock is suspended from trading on its principal market for ten consecutive trading days or for
more than an aggregate of 20 trading days in any 365-day period, or ceases to be listed on its principal market (a “Trading Failure”),
each holder may require the Company to redeem all or any portion of its Series C Preferred Stock in cash at a price equal to the greater
of (i) the Stated Value of such Series C Preferred Stock and (ii) the product of (x) the lowest Conversion Price in effect during the
period beginning on the date immediately preceding such Trading Failure and ending on the date of the holder’s redemption notice
and (y) the number of shares of Common Stock into which such Series C Preferred Stock is then convertible (without regard to any limitations
on conversion). 
 Fundamental
Transactions. In connection with certain fundamental transactions, each holder may elect either to convert all of its Series C Preferred
Stock into Common Stock, contingent upon the consummation of such fundamental transaction, or to receive, upon any subsequent conversion,
the alternate consideration receivable in such fundamental transaction by a holder of the number of shares of Common Stock into which
such Series C Preferred Stock was convertible immediately prior thereto.
