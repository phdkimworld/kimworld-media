 ChronoScale
Announces New Development Partnership to Accelerate AI Compute Capacity Deployment, Supported by a Significant Capital Raise 
 Initial deployment for a leading
frontier AI lab 
 The
initial deployment carries a total contract value of $7.5 billion over six years 
 ● Up
 to $750 million financing with participation by four strategic investors, including among
 others, Nutanix, Empery Digital Inc., and a storage infrastructure company 
 ● Financing
 brings together blue chip participants across the AI infrastructure ecosystem 
 MENLO
PARK, Calif., November 18, 2026 — ChronoScale Holdings Corporation (Nasdaq: CHRN) (“ChronoScale” or the
“Company”), an accelerated compute platform purpose-built to support demanding artificial intelligence workloads, today announced
that it has entered into definitive agreements to form a 50/50 development partnership with STN, Inc. to develop its Odessa, Texas site and a $750 million financing
including strategic investors. The partnership’s site will house a large-scale AI infrastructure deployment with a leading frontier
AI lab. The agreement contemplates AI infrastructure capacity across three phases with the first phase ordered and
scheduled for deployment in the first half of calendar year 2027. GPU configurations vary by phase. On a blended basis across all three
phases, the agreement represents approximately $20.8 million of annual revenue per megawatt. Each of the Company and STN will contribute
$390 million in value to the partnership in exchange for a 50% interest, subject to HSR clearance. 
 A
substantial portion of the first phase is backstopped by credit support from an investment-grade technology company and
is expected to provide up to $7.5 billion in total contract value (“TCV”) to the partnership over six years. 
 “We
continue to see frontier AI moving beyond incremental compute expansion toward infrastructure commitments measured in hundreds of megawatts,”
said Cenly Chen, Chief Executive Officer of ChronoScale. “We believe delivering at this scale requires more than access to technology.
Capital, strategic partnerships, and operational capabilities are critical to bring complex deployments online in a reliable manner.
This new agreement and financing, along with the support of our key strategic investors represents to us the confidence placed in our
ability to execute and establish a strong foundation for ChronoScale’s continued growth.” 
 $750
Million Financing 
 The
financing consists of $250 million in equity and an up to $500 million convertible preferred facility. A portion of the common equity
will be initially issued pursuant to a note structure held by Applied Digital Corporation, subject to conversion into underlying shares
of common equity in Q4 2026. The convertible preferred facility is structured with an initial minimum conversion price of $12 per share
and may be drawn by the Company over a four-year period, subject to certain customary draw conditions for facilities of this type. Upon
closing, the Company expects to draw $10 million under the convertible preferred facility, with the remaining capacity
providing ChronoScale with additional financing to support future deployment requirements. 
 Four
strategic investors are participating in the financing: a leading frontier AI lab, Nutanix, Empery Digital Inc., and a storage
infrastructure company. The equity investments to be made by these blue chip partners, as well as other institutional investors,
collectively underscore, for us the strategic importance of ChronoScale bringing together the technology, capital and infrastructure
capabilities required to deliver AI compute at scale. 
 The
equity financing is intended to support ChronoScale’s phased infrastructure deployment strategy under the development
partnership, while the convertible preferred facility is intended to provide financial flexibility as the Company scales its operations
to meet accelerating customer demand. 
 Goldman
Sachs & Co. LLC is serving as exclusive capital markets advisor to ChronoScale and is acting as lead placement agent for the financing.
Northland Capital Markets is acting as co-placement agent for the financing. Lake Street Capital Markets and B. Riley Securities are
serving as senior financial advisors, and Lucid Capital Markets and Needham & Company are serving as financial advisors, to ChronoScale. 
 Development
Partnership 
 The
partnership will be a collaboration between the Company and STN, Inc., the initial developer of the data center campus located
in Odessa, Texas. The first 65 MW of the planned deployment of this campus is scheduled to be completed in accordance with the deployment
schedule. The Company has a right of first offer on the remaining 285 MW of capacity at the campus, to support the second and
third phases of the agreement, if those are ordered. The proceeds of the Company’s common equity financing will fund the initial
portion of its contribution only, with the balance to be funded from cash on hand, other existing sources of capital, and/or future project
financing. The Company will manage the partnership in cooperation with representatives from STN. The Company will initially be
entitled to 50% of the profits and losses from the partnership based on its initial common equity ownership. The closing of the partnership
and the financing are each expected to occur on November 18, 2026, subject to the satisfaction or waiver of the closing conditions. 
 Phased
Deployment and Long-Term Growth 
 ChronoScale
is building an end-to-end AI infrastructure ecosystem designed to bring together hyperscalers, storage innovators, hardware manufacturers,
and AI software companies. Through its expanding AI infrastructure footprint and strategic partnerships, the Company is focused on delivering
dedicated, production-scale AI environments which it believes are optimized for training, inference, and high-performance computing. 
 We
believe the development partnership marks another significant step in ChronoScale’s strategy to establish itself
as a global provider of large-scale AI infrastructure, supporting the rapidly expanding compute requirements of frontier AI developers
and enterprise customers. 
 About
ChronoScale
