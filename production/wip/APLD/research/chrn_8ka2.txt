 EXPLANATORY
NOTE 
 ChronoScale
Holdings Corporation is filing this Amendment No. 2 to its Current Report on Form 8-K (this “Amendment”), originally
filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”) on November 18, 2026 (the “Original 8-K”), as
amended by Amendment No. 1 to the Original 8-K, filed with the SEC on November 18, 2026 (“Amendment No. 1”), solely to
supplement the disclosure included in the Original 8-K and to file Exhibits 4.1, 4.2, 10.5, 10.6 and 10.7. Except as set forth below, this Amendment does not amend or change any of the information previously disclosed
in the Original 8-K or Amendment No. 1. Interested parties should refer to the Original 8-K for Items 1.01, 2.03, 3.02, 5.03, 5.07 and
8.01 and Exhibits 3.1, 4.3, 10.1, 10.2, 10.3, 10.4 and 99.1 filed pursuant to Item 9.01 thereof, and to Amendment No. 1 for Item 8.01
and Exhibit 99.2 filed pursuant to Item 9.01 thereof. Capitalized terms used herein have the respective meanings ascribed thereto in the
Original 8-K unless otherwise defined herein. 
 Item 8.01. Other Matters. 
 On
November 18, 2026, the Company closed the transactions contemplated by the previously announced Common Stock Purchase Agreement and
issued (i) to the Common Stock Purchasers approximately 19.1 million shares of Common Stock for aggregate gross proceeds of $153.0
million and (ii) the Convertible Note to APLD. In addition, on November 18, 2026, the Company delivered an initial put
notice to the Investor for the purchase of shares of Series C Preferred Stock having an aggregate purchase price of $10.0 million. 
 Furthermore,
on November 18, 2026, the Contribution Closing
occurred and, in connection therewith, (i) ChronoScale Member and STN Member entered into the A&R LLCA, (ii) the Partnership
issued the Partnership Convertible Note to ChronoScale Member, (iii) the Partnership entered into the Term Loan Agreement with
Macquarie, (iv) the Partnership became the provider under the GPU Services Agreement, (v) the Company issued the STN Warrant to STN
and (vi) the Company entered into the Note Assignment Agreement with APLD Intermediate.
