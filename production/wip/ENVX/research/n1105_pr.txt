 Page 1 of 4 Enovix Organizes for Drone/Military Surge FREMONT, Calif., November 5, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Enovix Corporation ("Enovix" or the "Company," Nasdaq: ENVX), a developer and manufacturer of advanced lithium-ion batteries, today announced that it has completed the first phase of the cost reduction plan described to shareholders in August by Executive Chairman T.J. Rodgers. Over the past week, the Company rebalanced its world-wide deployment to put more focus on its South Korean operations, in both headcount and capital deployment, while reducing costs in the U.S., India and Malaysia. The change was a reduction of approximately 220 employee and contract-labor positions out of 946 with savings of $8.5 million per year. In Korea, the Company’s TAA-compliant, military-qualified plant is ramping to meet strong demand from U.S. and allied drone and defense programs for non-Chinese batteries. Enovix is investing $19 million to double the plant’s drone battery capacity to serve its rapidly growing drone, defense and industrial pipeline, which ballooned to $216 million at the end of Q3’26. A multinational provider of dual-use drone systems placed an initial purchase order in October for 100,000 battery units, with deliveries scheduled in early 2027 and expectations for follow-on orders in the second half of 2027 and beyond. The drone and defense purchase orders received in the first weeks of Q4’26 have already exceeded the Company’s total Q3’26 revenue. The Company believes the underlying growth in drone and defense demand for non-Chinese batteries will continue throughout the decade. T.J. Rodgers, Enovix Executive Chairman, said, “Our streamlining effort is intended to prevent the rapid hiring for the new opportunities from actually increasing costs. We are keeping a lid on costs in every other location and investing in Korea, where we expect 2027 revenue to be a multiple of 2026 revenue.” In Malaysia, the Company is focusing its Fab2 operations on fulfilling a production order for its advanced 100% active silicon-anode AI-1TM batteries from its lead smart eyewear customer, as well as on product development and sampling for smartphone, smart eyewear, wearable and other edge-AI customers.
