 1 Drones Drive Strong ENVX Revenue Growth New 2027 Revenue Guidance $65 Million to $85 Million $128 Million of 2030 Notes Retired Capturing a 36% Discount Restructuring Lowers Annual Cost by $8.5 Million FREMONT, Calif., November 12, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Enovix Corporation (“Enovix” or the “Company,” Nasdaq: ENVX), a developer and manufacturer of advanced lithium-ion batteries, today reported Q3’26 financial results, including a record non-GAAP gross margin this quarter of 34.5%, up 13.5 points. Fellow Shareholders: Our Q3’26 revenue was $11.0 million, up 38% from Q3’25 and 22% from Q2’26, our sixth consecutive quarter of year-over-year revenue growth. The higher gross margin resulted from a richer revenue mix, including smart eyewear revenue and drone and defense revenue.  2 Drone and defense customers again drove the quarter. Our drone, defense and industrial pipeline (identified prospects through design wins) grew 18% to approximately $216 million, up from $183 million in the prior quarter with drones accounting for $150 million of that. The drone pipeline spans the full range of unmanned platforms including – unmanned aerial vehicles (UAV), unmanned underwater vehicles (UUV), electric vertical takeoff and landing (eVTOL) aircraft, and unmanned ground vehicles (UGV) – served by our conventional graphite cells and our higher- energy silicon-blended graphite cells. Our 2027 revenue backlog received a boost with an initial 100,000-battery purchase order from a multinational provider of dual-use drone systems for conventional cells, and we also received an initial production order for our new MX1-B01 high- energy aerial drone cells from a U.S. drone manufacturer following successful field trials – with initial deliveries scheduled in early 2027. Steve Bakos, newly appointed Chief Revenue Officer and General Manager of Enovix, said, “The 100,000-battery order and the first MX1-B01 production order reflect our ability to convert our pipeline into orders. The MX1-B01 has now been sampled by more than 30 industry-leading aerial drone accounts with very positive feedback. Our customers need high-performance cells from a qualified, non-Chinese source, and they need them in volume next year. My team is focused on meeting that demand and helping define next-generation products.” Our $19 million expansion to double drone battery capacity at our military-qualified South Korean facility is underway and expected to come online in Q2’27. We will make further Korean capacity investments that track our drone and military demand. Our restructuring in early November included a 220-position reduction that cut approximately $8.5 million of annual cost and rebalanced our resources toward Korean operations where our near-term revenue opportunities continue to expand. Since quarter end, we have also retired $128 million of our 4.75% Convertible Senior Notes due 2030 (the “2030 Notes”) for approximately $81 million, capturing an approximately $47 million market discount, which will show up as profit in the GAAP, but not non-GAAP statements. We have also launched a warrant dividend designed to retire our 3.00% Convertible Senior Notes due 2028 (the “2028 Notes”). Finally, we are now guiding our fiscal year 2027 revenue at $65 million to $85 million, at the midpoint nearly double our expected 2026 revenue. 
 3 In smart eyewear, we brought our year-to-date shipments as of today to approximately 19,000 units against a 50,000-pack backlog that will be completed in the first half of 2027, and we continue to sample AI-2TM units to eyewear customers who need the second-generation energy density. In smartphones, our AI-1TM cells finally passed cell-level qualification testing internally, while our lead OEM customer is expected to successfully finish its parallel qualification next week. Unfortunately, the qualification was on the pre AI-2 version of our process to get going early and did not meet the latest energy density target of more than 1,000 watt-hrs/liter. As a result, we have decided not to proceed with system-level field testing using the pre AI-2 units and are concentrating our silicon-anode development on smart eyewear, where we already have backlog. Our next energy-density gains, including AI-2, will go first to a group of our 24 smart eyewear customers. Ryan Benton, Interim CEO, said, “Enovix is aligning its resources and cost structure with our strongest near-term opportunities. We are thus concentrating our silicon-anode development on smart eyewear and restructuring to direct more resources toward our operations in Korea. Our growing backlog and pipeline, coupled with our Korean capacity expansion, support our new outlook. For full-year 2027 revenue, we are guiding $65 million to $85 million, with nearly half the midpoint already in backlog.” Capital Structure Improvements We have repurchased $6.2 million of our common stock in Q3’26 and another $15.5 million in October. In addition, we have retired $128.0 million face value of 2030 Notes for approximately $81.4 million – a 36% discount that cut our debt by $46.6 million more than we paid. The remaining 2030 Notes stand at $232.0 million. We declared a dividend of one warrant for every four shares of common stock to stockholders on October 28, 2026, with a record date of tomorrow, November 13, 2026. Each warrant buys one share at $3.00, payable in cash or in 2028 Notes at full face value and expires April 28, 2028 – or earlier if our stock trades at or above $3.30 for 20 of any 30 consecutive trading days. Holders of 2028 Notes and 2030 Notes on the record date receive warrants on the same terms, without converting, based on the shares into which their notes were convertible. We expect to distribute 
 4 the warrants on or about November 18, 2026, and begin trading on Nasdaq as ENVXW. We cannot predict whether or when holders will exercise. Shareholders must own the shares at the end of trading on November 13 to gain the benefit. Conversely, anyone who has sold shares they do not own will owe the warrants for those shares to the lender on that date. T.J. Rodgers, Enovix Executive Chairman, concluded, “In our August press release I promised shareholders to reduce our expenses and to make our reports to investors more candid and transparent. This report is our first effort to move toward those standards.”

About Enovix Enovix develops and manufactures advanced lithium-ion batteries, including proprietary silicon- anode architectures for smartphones, smart eyewear, defense, industrial and emerging edge-AI applications. Its proprietary silicon-anode battery architecture enables higher energy density and performance in space-constrained devices while maintaining safety and reliability, supporting commercialization across consumer and industrial markets. Enovix is headquartered in Silicon Valley with facilities in India, South Korea and Malaysia, serving customers globally. For more information visit https://enovix.com and follow us on LinkedIn.

Note Regarding Customer Pipeline, Design Wins and Backlog We may refer in this press release and other communications to our “customer pipeline” and “design wins.” Our customer pipeline represents our estimate of the peak annual production value of identified design opportunities for products manufactured in South Korea. A “design win” refers to an opportunity that has been awarded to Enovix but has not yet entered production. Customer pipeline and design win amounts do not represent customer orders, backlog or committed revenue and should not be viewed as forecasts of future revenue. Actual revenue, if any, will depend on a number of factors, including customer qualification, final program awards, production timing, capacity and volumes, and the successful launch and ramp of customer programs. We also refer to our “backlog,” which represents orders we have received for delivery in future periods. Backlog is subject to modification, delay, reduction or cancellation.
