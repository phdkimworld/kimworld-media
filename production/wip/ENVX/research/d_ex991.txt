 Enovix Distributes Dividend of Warrants to Stockholders 
 FREMONT, Calif., November 18, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — Enovix
Corporation (“Enovix” or the “Company,” Nasdaq: ENVX), a developer and manufacturer of advanced lithium-ion batteries,
today announced that it will distribute warrants to purchase Enovix common stock (“Warrants”) to its stockholders and certain
convertible noteholders on November 18, 2026 (the “Distribution Date”), in accordance with the previously declared stockholder
warrant dividend. As previously announced, each stockholder of record as of the close of business on November 13, 2026 (the “Record
Date”) will receive one (1) Warrant for every four (4) shares of Enovix common stock held, rounded down to the nearest whole Warrant. 
 “Our first warrant dividend in 2025 demonstrated the strong support
of our stockholders and their interest in participating directly in Enovix’s growth,” said Ryan Benton, Interim Chief Executive
Officer. “This distribution builds on that by giving investors another way to participate while helping us address our outstanding
debt by permitting Warrant exercises with the delivery of our 3.00% Convertible Senior Notes due 2028 (the “2028 Notes”).
Accepting our 2028 Notes at face value as payment of the exercise price allows us to retire debt and preserve cash for the business, while
cash exercises provide additional funding that we could also apply toward retiring outstanding 2028 Notes and preserving flexibility to
support execution on our priorities.” 
 Warrant Terms 
 Eligibility: Stockholders must have purchased or held shares
no later than November 12, 2026 to be a stockholder of record as of the close of business on the November 13, 2026 Record Date and to
receive Warrants. 
 Convertible Noteholders: Holders of our 2028 Notes and our 4.75%
Convertible Senior Notes due 2030 (the “2030 Notes”) as of the Record Date will also receive Warrants on a pass-through basis,
as provided under the applicable indentures, at the same one-for-four ratio. 
 Receipt of Warrants: Eligible recipients do not need to take
any action or make any payment to receive their Warrants. Holders may exercise, hold or sell the Warrants. Holders whose securities are
held through an intermediary should contact that intermediary with questions about when the Warrants will appear in their accounts. 
 Cash Exercise Price: Each Warrant entitles the holder to purchase
one (1) share of Enovix common stock for $3.00 in cash, subject to adjustments and amendments under the warrant agreement. 
 Note Exercise Alternative: In lieu of cash, holders may exercise
333 Warrants by delivering $1,000 principal amount of 2028 Notes. Unless otherwise permitted by Enovix, the 2030 Notes may not be used
to exercise the Warrants. 
 Listing: The Warrants are expected to begin trading separately
from Enovix common stock on Nasdaq under the symbol ENVXW on November 19, 2026, subject to applicable approvals. 
 Scheduled Expiration: April 28, 2028, at 5:00 p.m. New York
City time, subject to early expiration and any extension under the warrant agreement. 
 Early Expiration Price Condition: This condition will be satisfied
if the daily volume-weighted average price (“VWAP”) of Enovix common stock equals or exceeds 110% of the then-applicable exercise
price, initially $3.30, on twenty (20) trading days within any thirty (30) consecutive trading-day period following issuance (the “Early
Expiration Price Condition”). The qualifying trading days need not be consecutive. 
 If the Early Expiration Price Condition is satisfied, the Warrants
will expire at 5:00 p.m. New York City time on the business day immediately following the final qualifying trading day, or a later date
selected by the Company in accordance with the warrant agreement. Enovix will announce the applicable expiration date by press release. 
 Exercise Using Cash or 2028 Notes 
 Beginning on the Distribution Date, holders may exercise their Warrants
using cash or 2028 Notes, subject to the warrant agreement. 
 The 2028 Notes are designated as eligible for exercise. The 2030 Notes
are not eligible and may not be delivered as payment. The Company may, in its discretion and subject to the notice requirements and other
terms of the warrant agreement, change the debt securities designated as eligible for exercise. 
 For cash exercises, each Warrant entitles the holder to purchase one
(1) share of Enovix common stock for $3.00. For exercises using 2028 Notes, holders may deliver $1,000 principal amount of 2028 Notes
for every 333 Warrants exercised and receive 333 shares. Note exercises must be submitted in whole multiples of this ratio, regardless
of the market price of the 2028 Notes. 
 For example, a holder delivering $20,000 principal amount of 2028 Notes
may exercise 6,660 Warrants and receive 6,660 shares of common stock. 
 The ability to exercise Warrants with 2028 Notes is available to any
investor holding 333 Warrants for each $1,000 principal amount of 2028 Notes delivered. Warrants may be acquired through the dividend
distribution or in the open market. Any investor may purchase additional Warrants in the market for exercise, and certain qualifying investors
may purchase additional 2028 Notes in market transactions. 
 Any 2028 Notes delivered and accepted for exercise of Warrants will
be cancelled, reducing Enovix’s outstanding debt and associated future interest obligations without requiring the Company to use
cash to repay that principal. Enovix will receive cash proceeds only from cash exercises of the Warrants. Any cash proceeds will be available
for general corporate purposes, including retiring 2028 Notes.

 Exercise Procedures and Investor Resources 
 Holders whose Warrants or 2028 Notes are held through a broker, bank
or other intermediary should contact that intermediary regarding receipt of their Warrants, exercise instructions, permitted payment methods
and applicable deadlines. 
 The early expiration feature could substantially shorten the exercise
period. Holders should make arrangements with their intermediaries in advance, as intermediaries may impose deadlines earlier than the
Company’s deadline. Warrants not exercised before the applicable expiration deadline will expire without value. 
 The warrant agreement, prospectus supplement and related investor materials
describe the complete terms, including exercise procedures, adjustments to the Warrants, any exercise suspension provisions and the conditions
governing early expiration. The warrant agreement and related registration documents were filed with the U.S. Securities and Exchange
Commission (“SEC”) on November 18, 2026. 
 Additional information, including frequently asked questions, is available
on the Company’s Investor Relations website at https://ir.enovix.com. 
 Transaction Advisors

on the structuring and distribution of the Warrants. 
 About Enovix 
 Enovix develops and manufactures advanced lithium-ion batteries, including
proprietary silicon-anode architectures for smartphones, smart eyewear, defense, industrial and emerging edge-AI applications. Its proprietary
silicon-anode battery architecture enables higher energy density and performance in space-constrained devices while maintaining safety
and reliability, supporting commercialization across consumer and industrial markets. 
 Enovix is headquartered in Silicon Valley with facilities in India,
South Korea and Malaysia, serving customers globally. For more information visit https://enovix.com and follow us on LinkedIn.
