Regulation FD Disclosure. On November 19, 2026, Plug Power Inc. (the “Company”) will host its eighth annual Plug Symposium virtually starting at 9:00 a.m. Eastern Time. This year's theme, “Strengthening Energy Security. Powering Energy Independence,” underscores hydrogen's critical role in enhancing global energy security. During the symposium, the Company’s senior leadership team, including Jose Luis Crespo, the Chief Executive Officer and President, and Paul B. Middleton, the Chief Financial Officer and Chief Accounting Officer, will present on the Company’s strategic vision and perspective on energy security and the role of hydrogen in addressing energy resilience and provide a financial update. The symposium will also include discussions and panel sessions with third-party partners, customers and industry experts. Topics to be addressed include hydrogen policy, the impact of the 2026 midterm elections on U.S. energy and hydrogen policy, emerging markets, project execution and financing, hydrogen applications, grid smoothing and distributed power, energy resilience and back-up power, and e-fuels and sustainable aviation fuel. The symposium will highlight the commercial launch of GenEco Scale, the Company’s recently announced integrated electrolyzer plant offering for utility-scale hydrogen production. A copy of the presentation materials that will be used at the symposium is furnished herewith as Exhibit 99.1 to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated herein by reference. A recording of the webcast will be available on the Company's website for a period of time following the symposium. The information included in this Item 7.01 and Exhibit 99.1 of this Current Report on Form 8-K is not deemed to be “filed” for purposes of Section 18 of the Securities Exchange Act of 1934, as amended (the “Exchange Act”), or otherwise subject to the liabilities of that section, nor shall this item or Exhibit 99.1 be incorporated by reference into the Company’s filings under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended, or the Exchange Act, except as expressly set forth by specific reference in such future filing.
